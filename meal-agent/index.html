meal-agent

A personal project by Luke Griffith

meal-agent is a small personal tool that runs on my own Raspberry Pi. It plans my weekly meals around my training, fills my grocery basket for review, and writes a weekly summary of my nutrition and progress.

It reads my own step count, weight and body composition from Google Health to include in that weekly summary. It is used only by me, with my own Google account. It is not a public service and doesn't accept other users.
